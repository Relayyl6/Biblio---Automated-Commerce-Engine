## The Core GTM Insight

**Merchants don't buy software. They buy solutions to pain they're feeling right now.**

The average informal merchant has tried 3–5 "solutions" and been burned by all of them. ACE cannot be sold as "AI-powered WhatsApp automation". It has to be sold as: **"Give me your WhatsApp number and I'll show you your next order getting processed without you typing a word."**

The product sells itself on demonstration. The GTM strategy is built around getting to that demo moment as fast as possible.

## The Beta Programme (50 Merchants)

**Target:** 50 merchants over Months 1–3. Curated selection, not open sign-ups.

**Selection criteria:**
- Processing ≥ 30 WhatsApp orders/month (enough data to show impact quickly)
- Already have WhatsApp Business number
- Has at least 3 product categories (tests Inventory Oracle)
- Has at least 1 active supplier relationship (tests restocking)
- Willing to do a 2-hour onboarding call

**Onboarding process:**
1. **1-hour voice onboarding** — ACE team calls the merchant. Connects WhatsApp Business number, bank account, preferred logistics partner. Calibrates Vendor Voice Profile (how the AI should sound like them). Sets autonomy boundaries.
2. **Week 1 shadow mode** — ACE observes all conversations, doesn't respond. Merchant reviews AI's "what I would have said" feed in the app. Builds trust.
3. **Week 2 partial automation** — ACE handles standard enquiries, payment confirmations, delivery updates. Merchant handles negotiations and new customer conversations.
4. **Week 3+ full automation** — All 4 workflows active. Merchant manages exceptions only.

**Beta pricing:** ₦0 for the first 3 months. After that, standard tier pricing. This removes all friction and lets the product prove itself.

## Acquisition Channels

### Channel 1: Market Partnerships (primary)

ACE will establish formal partnerships with market associations — organisations that represent thousands of merchants within a single physical market.

| Market | Location | Est. Member Merchants | Product Type |
|--------|---------|----------------------|-------------|
| Balogun Market Association | Lagos Island | 8,500+ | Fashion, fabrics |
| Yaba Market | Yaba, Lagos | 3,200+ | Fabrics, accessories |
| Computer Village Merchants | Ikeja | 2,100+ | Electronics |
| Idumota Market | Lagos Island | 5,000+ | FMCG, personal care |

**Partnership model:**
- ACE pays market association: ₦500 per merchant onboarded
- Market association endorses ACE in their newsletter, WhatsApp group, and market meetings
- ACE provides the association with aggregate (anonymised) data on member merchants' performance
- Association benefits: their members grow revenue → they have stronger membership

### Channel 2: Merchant-Referral Programme

| Referral Action | Reward |
|----------------|--------|
| Merchant refers another merchant who activates | 1 month free subscription |
| Referred merchant stays 3+ months | ₦5,000 cash credit |
| Merchant refers 5+ merchants who activate | Lifetime Growth tier upgrade |

**Why this works:** Trust in informal markets is relationship-based. A merchant won't try software recommended by a stranger. They will absolutely try it if their market neighbour is getting results from it.

### Channel 3: Instagram/WhatsApp Content

The target customer is already on social media. ACE's content strategy is:

1. **"Behind the business" case studies** — Real merchants (with permission) showing their ACE Command Center with real numbers. "From 6hrs/day managing WhatsApp to 20 minutes/day. ₦180K more revenue last month."
2. **AI negotiation threads** — Show real (anonymised) negotiation transcripts. "Customer: ₦16K. AI: Here's how it responded..." Highly shareable in merchant communities.
3. **WhatsApp group seeding** — Every merchant association, fashion business group, and food distribution WhatsApp group gets a weekly case study drop from a relatable merchant voice.

### Channel 4: Direct Sales (SDR Team)

- Month 4+: 2 SDRs focused on Growth and Scale tier prospects
- Target: merchants already using Paystack + logistics services (signal they're ready for automation)
- Demo-first approach: "Let me show you what would have happened with your last 3 orders"

## The Demo Script

The single most powerful acquisition tool is a **live demonstration of the order fulfillment workflow** using the prospect's actual WhatsApp number and a test order.

```
Duration: 8 minutes

Step 1 (2 min): Show them their current process — how many messages, how many apps
  "Last week you said you spent 5 hours on WhatsApp. Let's count the steps."

Step 2 (4 min): Run a test order through ACE on their phone
  - Salesperson texts the merchant's WhatsApp as a customer: "Abeg I need 2 of the Ankara"
  - ACE responds — in the merchant's dialect and style
  - ACE requests payment to virtual account
  - (simulated) payment confirmed
  - ACE sends delivery update
  Total ACE activity: 6 messages. Merchant typed: 0.

Step 3 (2 min): Show the exception that would have required their input
  "Here's the only thing ACE couldn't handle — the customer wanted 40% off.
   ACE sent you an SMS: 'Reply 1 to approve, 2 to decline, 3 to offer bundle.'
   You replied 2. ACE handled the rest. That was 8 seconds of your time."

Close: "That's every order, every day, while you sleep."
```

## Expansion Roadmap

### Phase 2: Nigeria-Wide (Month 12–24)

**Cities:** Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, Ibadan, Enugu

**Expansion triggers:**
- Lagos merchants at > 2,000 active (network effect building)
- Logistics aggregator has enough volume for ₦350/delivery tier
- Global Buyer ID has > 50,000 customer profiles (cross-merchant checkout working)

**Channel expansion:**
- FMCG distributor partnerships (they have relationships with 1,000s of informal retailers)
- Radio advertising in Hausa markets (Kano) and Igbo markets (Aba, Onitsha)
- Dialect-native onboarding: full Hausa and Igbo language support

### Phase 3: West Africa + Kenya (Year 3)

| Market | Language / Dialect | WhatsApp Commerce Size | ACE Adaptation Needed |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Ghana | English + Twi | $42B | Logistics (Jumia/Glovo APIs) |
| Kenya | English + Swahili + Sheng | $67B | M-Pesa payment integration |
| Côte d'Ivoire | French + Nouchi | $31B | French intent parser |
| Senegal | French + Wolof | $18B | French intent parser |

**Kenya is priority:** M-Pesa integration makes payment verification dramatically simpler than Nigeria's bank-API approach. Swahili dialect BERT is more developed than Yoruba/Hausa. WhatsApp penetration is extremely high.
