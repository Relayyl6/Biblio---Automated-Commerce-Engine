# Enterprise Products — Strategic Overview

> ACE's secondary business. The true moat and exponential value multiplier.

---

## The Thesis

ACE's primary B2C/B2B2C platform makes ACE a **real-time sensor network across the most opaque economy on Earth**.

We're capturing data that doesn't exist in structured form anywhere else:
- Hyper-localised demand signals
- Conversational commerce patterns in 47 dialects
- Alternative credit behaviour of the unbanked
- Real-time FMCG product movement at street level
- Pricing elasticity in informal markets

**Ungoogleable. Unscrapeable. Unimaginably valuable.**

## The Three Products

### [Product 1: AI Training Data Marketplace](../../data-intelligence/ai-training-marketplace/)
**Buyers:** OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta AI, Cohere, Mistral  
**What they pay for:** Non-Western, dialect-rich, real-world conversational commerce data that doesn't exist anywhere else  
**Moat:** Only source of Nigerian Pidgin / Yoruba / Hausa / Igbo commerce data at scale  
**Year 2 ARR:** $7.2M (8 clients @ avg $75K/month)

---

### [Product 2: FMCG Market Intelligence](../../data-intelligence/fmcg-intelligence/)
**Buyers:** Unilever, Nestlé, P&G, PZ Cussons, Dangote Group, Chi Limited  
**What they pay for:** Real-time demand signals, price elasticity, distribution gap maps — with 0-week lag vs their current 6–8 week lag  
**Moat:** Only data source operating at the point of sale in informal markets  
**Year 2 ARR:** $5.76M (12 clients @ avg $40K/month)

---

### [Product 3: ACE TrustScore API](../../data-intelligence/trust-score-api/)
**Buyers:** Kuda, FairMoney, Carbon, GTBank, Access Bank, MFIs, BNPL platforms  
**What they pay for:** Alternative credit scores for 180M people locked out of formal credit  
**Moat:** 73% correlation to loan repayment (28% better than traditional bureau scores for informal merchants)  
**Year 2 ARR:** $3.57M (850K checks/month @ $0.35 avg)

## The Go-To-Market Sequence

**Year 1:** Primary focus is merchant onboarding. Data collection begins as a by-product. No enterprise sales yet.  
**Year 2:** Data Refinement Pipeline (Airflow) operational. First enterprise contracts for AI labs and one FMCG pilot.  
**Year 3:** Full enterprise product suite live. TrustScore API in production. FMCG dashboard for multiple brands.  
**Year 4–5:** Proprietary ASR API launched. Enterprise revenue overtakes merchant subscription revenue.

## Enterprise Buyer Discovery Questions

*(To validate product-market fit before building)*

**AI Labs:**
- What % of your model's errors occur on African/Pidgin inputs?
- What would you pay for a 50K annotated conversation dataset in Nigerian Pidgin with intent tags?

**FMCG:**
- How long is your current sell-through data lag?
- What decisions are you making blind due to informal market opacity?

**Banks:**
- What % of SME loan applications are rejected due to insufficient credit history?
- What is your current loss rate on informal merchant loans?
