ACE is an invisible operating system for informal commerce. We turn WhatsApp chaos into autonomous businesses through radical AI automation. Merchants keep texting customers on WhatsApp — our AI handles inventory, payments, logistics, and customer retention completely in the background. No new apps for customers. No manual work for merchants. Just autonomous growth.

---

## Elevator Pitch — 2 Minutes (Investor Version)

In Nigeria and across emerging markets, $2.3 trillion in commerce happens entirely on WhatsApp. No Shopify, no POS systems — just chaotic text messages, voice notes in Pidgin, and screenshots of bank transfers. These merchants are growing 40% year-over-year but hit a hard ceiling at $50K annual revenue because they can't scale past their personal bandwidth.

ACE is their invisible back-office. We're not another chat aggregator — we're a fully autonomous commerce engine. Our AI doesn't assist; it executes. It handles supplier reordering, payment reconciliation via banking APIs, dynamic pricing negotiations, and logistics coordination without human input. The merchant's only job becomes exception management.

But here's the real play: By processing millions of informal transactions, we're building the most valuable dataset in emerging markets — real-time consumer demand signals, alternative credit scores, and hyper-localised language models. Our secondary business sells this refined intelligence to three enterprise buyers: AI labs desperate for non-Western training data, FMCG brands flying blind in informal distribution, and banks who can't underwrite the unbanked.

We're Scale AI meets Respond.io, built specifically for the $2.3T informal economy that traditional SaaS completely ignores.
