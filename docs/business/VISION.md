These merchants are growing 40% year-over-year but hit a hard ceiling at $50K annual revenue because they can't scale past their personal bandwidth.

ACE is their invisible back-office. We're not another chat aggregator — we're a fully autonomous commerce engine. Our AI doesn't assist; it executes. When a customer messages "Abeg I need that blue dress from your last reel", our AI resolves which product they mean from Instagram, negotiates the price like a skilled market trader, issues a virtual bank account, verifies payment, books a dispatch rider, and updates inventory — all before the merchant has even opened the app. The merchant's only job becomes exception management, via a single SMS reply.

But here's the real play: By processing millions of informal transactions, we're building the most valuable dataset in emerging markets — real-time consumer demand signals, street-level SKU sell-through data, negotiation traces with price elasticity at the product level, alternative credit scores for 180 million unbanked people, and hyper-localised language models in 47 African dialects. Our secondary business sells this refined intelligence to three enterprise buyers: AI labs desperate for non-Western training data, FMCG brands flying blind in informal distribution, and banks who can't underwrite the unbanked.

We are Scale AI meets Respond.io, built specifically for the $2.3T informal economy that traditional SaaS completely ignores.

## The Two Differentiators That Stand Alone

### 1. The AI Negotiator

No competitor has an autonomous negotiation agent. ACE's AI Negotiator operates like a skilled market trader — it reads the customer's full relationship history, opens at the right anchor price, uses dialect-appropriate tactics (Relationship Anchor, Bundle Pivot, Scarcity Signal, Future Credit, Urgency Window), and closes deals within merchant-defined boundaries without any merchant input.

The **Rust circuit breaker** ensures the AI is physically prevented from going below the pricing floor — no matter what a customer types, including injection attacks.

Every negotiation arc generates a `NegotiationTrace` — price elasticity data per SKU, per geography, per customer tier — which becomes a premium data asset sold to FMCG buyers.

### 2. The Vendor Communiqué System

When an exception falls outside the AI's autonomous authority, ACE doesn't require the merchant to open an app or navigate a dashboard. It sends an **SMS with a single-digit reply protocol**:

```
ACE: Amaka wants dress at ₦12K (your floor: ₦14,250).
     Reply 1=approve, 2=hold firm, 3=offer bundle
```

The merchant replies with "2" from any phone — including a basic feature phone — and the AI executes the decision. This makes ACE the only commerce automation tool that works for merchants who are at the market, in traffic, or away from their phone all day.
